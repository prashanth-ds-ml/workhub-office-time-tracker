# Welcome to WorkHub

WorkHub is our team's attendance and time-tracking tool. This guide covers everything you need: creating your account, and setting up the browser reminder so you never forget to punch in or out.

Takes about 5 minutes total.

---

## Part 1: Create your account

1. Open **[workhub-office-time-tracker.vercel.app](https://workhub-office-time-tracker.vercel.app)** in your browser.
2. Click **"New to WorkHub? Create an account"**.
3. Fill in:
   - **Full name**
   - **Company email** (your `@sims.healthcare` address)
   - **Password** (at least 6 characters — pick your own, this is your private password)
   - **Account type**: choose **Employee**
   - **Security question** and **Security answer**: pick something only you'd know (e.g. "What street did you grow up on?"). **Remember this** — it's how you reset your password later if you forget it. There is no "email me a code" option, so if you forget both your password and your answer, you'll need to ask an admin to help.
4. Click **Create account**. You're in.

## Part 2: Using WorkHub day-to-day

- **Punch in** when you start work (Office or Home), **punch out** when you're done, for the day. Use the bar at the bottom of the screen.
- The **Calendar** tab shows your attendance history, color-coded: green = on time, orange = late, red = missed.
- **Attendance** tab shows your full session history for the month.
- **Announcements** tab has company updates.

If you ever forget your password: click **Forgot password?** on the login screen, enter your email, and answer your security question.

## Part 3: Install the punch-in/out reminder (recommended)

This is a small Microsoft Edge extension that pops up a reminder if you forget to punch in (between 10:45–11:00 AM) or forget to punch out (between 5:30–6:00 PM). One-time setup, about 2 minutes.

1. You should have received a file called **`workhub-punch-reminder-extension-protected.zip`** along with this guide. It's password-protected (Gmail blocks unprotected zips like this one).
2. Double-click it to unzip. When it asks for a password, enter: **`WorkHub2026!`**
   - If Windows' built-in unzip tool refuses to open it or gives an error, install the free **[7-Zip](https://www.7-zip.org/)**, then right-click the file → 7-Zip → Extract Here, and enter the password there instead.
3. Open Edge and go to `edge://extensions` in the address bar.
4. Turn on **Developer mode** (toggle in the bottom-left of the page).
5. Click **Load unpacked**.
6. Select the unzipped folder (the one with `manifest.json` inside it).
7. You should now see the WorkHub icon in your toolbar.
8. Make sure you have a WorkHub tab open and are logged in — this lets the extension know who you are.

That's it — you're all set. You won't need to touch it again unless told there's an update.

**To test it works:** click the WorkHub extension icon in your toolbar, then click **"Preview reminder popup"** — you should see the reminder pop up immediately.
